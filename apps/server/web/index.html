AppRelay

Sign iOS IPA files in your browser

Sign in with your Apple account, pair your iPhone with AppRelay VPN, and choose an .ipa file to sign.

Open signing workspace

Upload an IPA from the command line

The AppRelay CLI uploads your IPA and prints a share link you can open in the signing workspace. Requires Node.js 24.12 or newer. 

npx @brosoft/apprelay ./MyApp.ipa
