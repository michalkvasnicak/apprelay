Share iOS development builds with AppRelay.

AppRelay is open source. Upload an IPA to get a share link, or sign and install a build on your own iPhone from the browser.

npx @brosoft/apprelay app.ipa

or sign and install an IPA

Share a build with a link.

Upload an .ipa with the CLI and send the link to your testers. Anyone with the link can download the IPA, but each tester needs to sign it for their own iPhone before installing it with AppRelay.

Pair your iPhone to install without a Mac.

You’ll need an iPhone running iOS 27 or later, with Developer Mode enabled and AppRelay VPN installed from TestFlight. Keep the iPhone on Wi-Fi with the VPN connected while pairing and installing. No Xcode is needed to install an existing build.

Sign the app in your browser.

Sign in with your Apple Account. You can use a free Apple development team for supported builds, subject to Apple’s provisioning limits. The browser signs the IPA for your paired iPhone, then uploads it so AppRelay can install it over the VPN.

Built for simple development builds.

Signing supports IPAs up to 100 MiB compressed and 256 MiB unpacked. Apps with extensions, embedded frameworks, custom capabilities, or encrypted binaries aren’t supported.
