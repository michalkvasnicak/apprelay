01 · SHARE

Send a link

Upload an .ipa with the CLI and send the link to testers. Each tester signs it for their own iPhone before installing. Links expire after 7 days, so they’re not a permanent archive.

02 · PAIR

Pair your iPhone

Enable Developer Mode, install AppRelay VPN from TestFlight, and stay on Wi-Fi with the VPN connected. No Xcode needed.

03 · SIGN

Sign in your browser

Sign in with a free or paid Apple Account. Both work, though free accounts are subject to Apple’s stricter provisioning limits. AppRelay installs over the VPN.
