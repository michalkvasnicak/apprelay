Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Brosoft s.r.o. (“Brosoft,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and discloses information when you use AppRelay websites, applications, software, command-line tools, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

The information we process depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • account information, such as your email address and sign-in information;
  • apps, files, and configurations you upload or share through the Service, including IPA files;
  • information needed to pair and sign for your device, such as device identifiers; and
  • communications with us, including support requests and feedback.

We may also collect information automatically when you use the Service, including device and browser information, IP address, identifiers, usage activity, connection information, diagnostics, crash data, and performance information.

Apple account credentials

Your browser uses your Apple Account to request signing assets from Apple for your device. AppRelay does not send or store your Apple password. Requests to Apple go through our HTTPS relay and can include your account identifier, verification code, and session tokens. When you set a passphrase, your Apple session and signing assets are saved in encrypted browser storage. Apple handles information it receives under its own privacy policy.

Shared builds and install uploads

Anyone with a share link can download the uploaded IPA without signing in. Share links only with people you want to have access. When you install an app, signing takes place in your browser, and the browser uploads the signed IPA so AppRelay can deliver it to your paired iPhone.

How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve the Service; authenticate users and devices; deliver shared apps; communicate with you; provide support; monitor performance; prevent fraud and abuse; protect the Service and its users; comply with law; and enforce our Terms of Use.

How we disclose information

We may disclose information to vendors that process it on our behalf, such as hosting, storage, analytics, and error-monitoring providers; to people with whom you choose to share an app link; when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security; in connection with a financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets; or with your consent. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

VPN traffic

AppRelay’s VPN carries traffic to pair with your iPhone and install apps. Browser signing and uploads do not go through the VPN. Other internet traffic is not routed through AppRelay. We do not inspect, retain, or sell the contents of VPN traffic, and process it only as technically necessary to transmit it.

Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the Service, remember preferences, understand usage, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function properly without them.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no security measure is perfect.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, in Slovakia the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov). Contact us to make a request. We may verify your identity before fulfilling it and will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

International use

Brosoft is based in Slovakia. If you use the Service elsewhere, information may be processed in the European Union and other countries where we or our providers operate, subject to applicable law.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.

Contact
