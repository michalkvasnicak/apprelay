Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Brosoft s.r.o., based in the Slovak Republic, is the controller responsible for personal data we process to operate AppRelay’s website, iPhone app, and command-line upload tool. You don’t need an AppRelay user account. Apple sign-in is used for app signing.

Apple sign-in and browser storage

Your browser uses your Apple Account to request signing assets from Apple. Your Apple password is used in your browser and is not sent to AppRelay or saved. Requests relayed to Apple can include your Apple Account identifier, verification code, and session tokens. The relay forwards those requests without saving their contents. The sign-in flow also connects to an Anisette service to obtain data required by Apple’s sign-in protocol. That service receives protocol data, not your Apple password or account tokens. Apple handles the information it receives under its privacy policy.

If you choose to save your Apple session and signing assets, AppRelay keeps them in passphrase-encrypted storage in your browser, not in an AppRelay account. You can remove the saved data in AppRelay or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Shared builds and install uploads

When you upload a build for sharing, we store the IPA and information about it, such as its filename, size, checksum, and share-link expiry, if any. Anyone with an active share link can download the file without signing in. Share the link only with people you want to have access.

App signing takes place in your browser. If you then choose to install the app, the browser uploads the signed IPA to Cloudflare R2 for delivery to your paired iPhone. AppRelay records the status of the installation. Shared builds are also stored in Cloudflare R2.

Pairing, VPN, and notifications

AppRelay processes device registration and pairing data, including your device’s VPN public key, assigned VPN address, device identifiers, and pairing records. The VPN connects AppRelay to your iPhone for pairing and installation. It does not route your general internet traffic. Browser signing and uploads do not go through the VPN.

If you enable notifications, AppRelay uses your device’s push token with Apple Push Notification service to deliver pairing and installation updates.

Service logs, analytics, and error reporting

AppRelay uses IP addresses to limit requests and protect the Service from abuse. AppRelay’s server logs record request details such as the route pattern, response status, and response time. If you email us, we receive your email address and message so we can reply.

Where enabled, AppRelay uses Umami to measure page views and steps in the pairing, sign-in, and installation flows. AppRelay reports generic page paths without share or pairing tokens, query strings, or referrers. Where enabled, Sentry receives error reports from the website, iPhone app, or server to help us diagnose failures.

Why we use and disclose data

We process data to provide the features you request and perform our agreement with you. We rely on our legitimate interests, where applicable, to secure and maintain AppRelay, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures. We also process data when required to meet legal obligations.

We disclose data as needed to operate AppRelay, including to hosting, storage, and monitoring services, and to people who have an active link to a build you share. We may disclose data when required by law. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention

Device registrations and pairing authorization expire. Shared builds with an expiry date stop being accessible through AppRelay after that date; storage cleanup may occur later. If no expiry is set, the shared build can remain stored indefinitely. The expiry of an install authorization is not a guarantee that the uploaded file has been deleted.

We keep operational data and correspondence as needed to operate and secure AppRelay, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, or receive it in a portable format, where those rights apply. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic .

Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes and provide additional notice when required by law.
